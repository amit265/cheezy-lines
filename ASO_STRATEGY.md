# 🚀 Cheezy Lines - App Store Optimization (ASO) Strategy

This document outlines the complete App Store Optimization strategy to maximize organic downloads on both the Apple App Store and Google Play Store for western audiences.

## 1. 🏷️ App Title & Subtitle
The title and subtitle hold the most weight for search engine ranking. Do NOT just use "Cheezy Lines". 

### Apple App Store
- **App Name (Max 30 chars):** Cheezy Lines: Pick Up & Rizz
- **Subtitle (Max 30 chars):** Flirty Quotes & Tinder Prompts

### Google Play Store
- **App Title (Max 30 chars):** Cheezy Lines: Rizz & Flirting
- **Short Description (Max 80 chars):** The ultimate wingman! Get the best pick up lines, rizz, and dating app prompts.

---

## 2. 🔑 Keywords (Apple App Store ONLY)
Apple provides a hidden 100-character keyword field. 
**Rules:** Separate by commas. No spaces after commas. Don't repeat words. Don't use the words already in your App Name.

**Use this exact string (97 chars):**
`tinder,hinge,bumble,dating,icebreaker,funny,jokes,wingman,flirt,romance,couples,crush,messages,ai`

## 5. ⭐ Ratings & Reviews Strategy
The iOS algorithm heavily favors apps with recent 5-star reviews.
- **Initial Boost:** Ask 5-10 friends or family members to download the app by searching for the exact keyword (e.g. "Cheezy Lines Rizz") rather than using a direct link. Have them leave a 5-star rating and a short text review.
- **In-App Prompt:** The app is already perfectly configured with `expo-store-review`! It will automatically ask engaged users to leave a rating natively without leaving the app.
